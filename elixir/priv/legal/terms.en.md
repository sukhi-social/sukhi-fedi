## What we care about here

Something I hope people who come here will quietly remember, for one another.

### 1. Wish the other person a little peace

The person in front of you thinks, feels and makes mistakes, just as you do. Try, in just one line, silently in your mind, to wish them a peaceful day. When you notice a difference between you, that wish doesn't have to change.

### 2. I make mistakes too

No one can be right all the time. When someone points something out, it means they've shown you something you couldn't see. The person who can say "Ah, I see. Thank you for telling me" is the strong one here.

### 3. Wait one breath before you write

What you write travels further than you think. It drops, without warning, into a day belonging to someone you don't know, on a timeline somewhere in the fediverse. Before you send, take just one breath, so your words don't unsettle someone's peace. That's all.

## A few requests

Everyone who comes here is one precious life. If we can treat one another this way, this will become a warmer place.

- **Look out for people who are new.**
  If a name you don't know shows up on your timeline, try saying "Welcome" just once. Some people don't like being spoken to, so it's fine to say it only in your heart.
- **Use a CW (content warning) for topics people may disagree about.**
  Politics, religion, social controversies. So that nobody freezes mid-sip over a glass of barley tea. One line on top saying what it's about would be much appreciated.
- **If two people have a misunderstanding, please try to keep it between the two of you.**
  A timeline is a place with an audience. When people talk in front of an audience, being watched stiffens the conversation more than the actual point does. Try a DM or another way of reaching each other first. If you still can't find common ground, keeping your distance with mute or block is also kind to both of you.

## What can't be posted here

I can't write everything out in detail. I'll think through where exactly the lines fall case by case.
But I ask that the following not be posted here.

- **Anything that breaks the law.**
  These are lines that society has already drawn. In some cases this means reporting to the relevant authorities or cooperating with an investigation.
- **Words that put someone below others.**
  So that everyone who comes here can feel at peace: please don't use words that put anyone beneath others because of where they come from, their position or how they look. That's true even if you're speaking from a minority position.
- **Behaviour that goes past someone's "no".**
  An exchange that continues after someone has asked for it to stop is harassment, not an exchange of views. If you can't see where the line is, ask the operator.
- **A flood of machine voices on the public timeline.**
  Please hold back a little, so that people's voices aren't buried under machines'.
- **Strong stimulation.** This is a place many eyes pass by without warning. Even with a CW, please don't post strong sexual or violent expression, or fast flashing or motion. Especially in profiles and display names.

We'll take our time thinking about how to respond, case by case. First, we talk.
